# **Food Oral Processing & Artificial inteligence**

Welcome to this e-book on combining food oral processing research with machine learning approaches.

## About This Book ##

This book documents workflows for:
- Fundamentals of Food Oral Processing
- Sensory perception modeling
- Computer vision applications in food science
- Neural network approaches for texture analysis
- Synthetic data generation for food research
